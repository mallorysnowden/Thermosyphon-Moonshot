## Case notes

- **A. AI data center, 100 MW IT (Fargo / Ellendale, ND).** Trim above what dry coolers can do at 32 degC supply / 42 degC return, 8 K approach. Electricity 7.5 c/kWh (ND industrial 7.25 c, EIA 2024). Median freezing degree-days 1315 degC-days; median-year cooling 22,022 MWh.
- **B. Hospital / campus, 3.5 MWth (Minneapolis, MN).** Water-cooled chiller plant, 3 x 50%, ~$1,500/ton, COP 4.7. Electricity 12.5 c/kWh (MN commercial 12.15 c). Median freezing degree-days 820 degC-days; median-year cooling 6,114 MWh.
- **C. Salmon processor, 1.5 MWth (Naknek, Bristol Bay, AK).** Ammonia refrigeration, COP 3. Electricity 45 c/kWh (diesel self-generation; Naknek fuel $6.75/gal). Ice alone chills to about +1 degC, not the -1 degC of refrigerated seawater. Median freezing degree-days 1322 degC-days; median-year cooling 993 MWh.
- **D. Year-round process cooling, 1 MWth at 5 degC (Fargo, ND).** BAU: chiller plus a dry-cooler economiser that cools for free below -3 degC. Median freezing degree-days 1315 degC-days; median-year cooling 8,760 MWh.
